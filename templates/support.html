Support

Common questions and quick help for using StockMate.

How do I add a product?

Open Add Product, enter the product details and click Save.

How do I edit stock?

Go to Inventory and click Edit beside the product.

How is GST calculated?

The billing page currently uses an 18% GST rate on the amount after discount.

Billing Help

Select products and quantities, choose a discount and payment method, then click Generate Bill.

Need more help?

Use the Contact page for support contact details.
